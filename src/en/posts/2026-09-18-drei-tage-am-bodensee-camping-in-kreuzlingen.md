From 18 to 20 September 2026 we were in Kreuzlingen with our Cali, right on Lake Constance. The weather was partly cloudy at a pleasant 17 to 23 degrees, perfect for a few quiet days of camping.

The pitch was reasonably sized and fairly priced at CHF 59 per night. We particularly liked the mix of grass and gravel, which turned out to keep the dust down nicely. With around 60 pitches the campsite is on the small side, which made it all the cosier. Everything felt very well kept.

For the children there was a playground as well as a great play corner with lots of games, puzzles and books. In the middle of the site there is a central barbecue area, which many campers used for sociable barbecue evenings.

Right next door is a beer garden with hearty food such as burgers and schnitzel. Also next door there is a lovely lido, which had its last day of the season on Saturday. A small supermarket at reception meant we did not have to walk far for the essentials.

On Saturday the campsite also held a small Oktoberfest with typical Bavarian culture and food. We gave it a miss, though, as we had not brought our traditional costumes. Instead, Andy left the site to run a half marathon at Lake Greifensee, while the rest of the family enjoyed the peace and quiet by the lake.

Our pitch was not the biggest, but the location right on Lake Constance, the proximity to the lido, beer garden and supermarket, and the friendly, family atmosphere made this stay in Kreuzlingen a thoroughly lovely camping experience. No lessons learnt this time, just a successful weekend by the lake.
