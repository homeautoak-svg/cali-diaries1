From 4 to 6 September 2026 we made a short stopover with our Cali at TCS Camping Disentis. The site is very close to nature, in a quiet location, and our pitch was pleasantly large. We paid CHF 46 per night, and thanks to good WIFI we could quickly look things up when needed.

On Friday the sky was sunny and at 29 degrees it was properly hot. We took the day easy and enjoyed the surroundings, the rushing of the river included.

On Saturday we set off on foot, walking about 40 minutes uphill to Disentis, which sits higher up. The weather that day was cloudier and, at 23 degrees, pleasantly mild for a walk. Disentis itself is a cosy little mountain village. For lunch we treated ourselves to pinsa and a strawberry ice cream before stocking up on snacks for the next few days at the local SPAR.

Back at the campsite we split the afternoon: partly at the water playground, partly down by the Vorderrhein. There we watched the gold panners at work for a while, which was an exciting experience, especially for the children. For dinner we went to the local restaurant.

The only small drawback: unfortunately there is no children's bathroom on the site. Otherwise the sanitary facilities were well kept and clean, and overall it was a really lovely, natural place to camp.

What we learnt for next time: pack jumpers! Even though it was still nice and hot on Friday, it got noticeably cooler in the mountains in the evening and on Saturday.
