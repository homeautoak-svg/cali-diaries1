We are Andy and Sarah, we live in Switzerland, and we have recently become the proud drivers of a VW T7 California. We have given ourselves two years to find out whether camping life suits us, and the first outings have already answered that question pretty clearly.

Our daughter comes along too. At three years old she is at exactly the age where every pitch is a new adventure and every meadow a voyage of discovery. Camping with a child brings its very own dynamic: different daily routines, different priorities when choosing a pitch, and lots of little moments that more than make up for the effort.

We are right at the start of our camping experience. Many things we are trying for the first time, some work straight away, others we only learn because they did not work the first time. And that is exactly what we want to write about here: our trips, the places we liked and the lessons we pick up along the way.

The blog deliberately accompanies us only as a look back at what we have already experienced, with everything that goes with it.

Great that you are stopping by. The first proper travel report is coming soon.
