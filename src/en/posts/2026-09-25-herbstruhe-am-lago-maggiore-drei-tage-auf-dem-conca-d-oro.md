Around 30 minutes beyond the Swiss border, on Lago Maggiore, lies a beautiful campsite: Conca D'Oro in Feriolo di Baveno. The site is divided into different areas, and we get a pitch right by the river and the lake, embraced by the surrounding mountains. On the drive from Zurich to Italy we switch to narrow roads along the lake for the last quarter and keep wanting to stop to enjoy the view.

It is the last open weekend of the season, and you can clearly feel that it is the off-season. So much calm really invites you to unwind. On Friday evening we go for pizza at the local restaurant. On Saturday we stroll along the lakeside promenade to nearby Feriolo and enjoy the mild, sunny weather at 22 to 25 degrees. For lunch we feast on tomato and mozzarella and pumpkin ravioli with sage butter.

There is plenty going on in the animal world too: ducks, swans and even beavers swim past our pitch, while rabbits hop across the meadow. Every now and then the church bells from the village remind us that another hour has passed.

On Saturday evening we enjoy Italian food once again, before packing up in the sunshine on Sunday morning. We are already looking forward to driving home along the lake.

The pitch was reasonably sized, the cost fair at CHF 32.10 per night, and we spent a total of CHF 148.02 on restaurant visits. The site scores with a playground, supermarket and WiFi, making it ideal for families too.

It was lovely. Perhaps autumn on an Italian lake should become a category of its own on this blog.
