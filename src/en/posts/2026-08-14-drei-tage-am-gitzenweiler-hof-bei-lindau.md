From 14 to 16 August 2026 our Cali was parked at Gitzenweiler Hof in Lindau on Lake Constance. The weather showed its full range: clear skies and a whopping 36 degrees, but on Saturday evening it rained after all.

The pitch itself was gravel, but it came with an electricity and water hook-up, which was all we needed. For food there were several options right on the site: an inn with hearty home cooking, a pizzeria serving pizza and pasta, and a bistro in the beer garden with snacks. There was also really good live music by Philipp Lumpp, which was a real highlight of our stay.

There was plenty on offer for children too: several new playgrounds, including a great nature playground in Native American style, plus a dedicated kids' entertainment team. The two new washhouses were modern and clean as well, and the children's bathroom with its elephant and jungle decor was really remarkably designed.

Handy: with the included Bodensee card we could travel to Lindau on public transport for free and got discounts on various admissions. Unfortunately we had not thought about the Assumption Day holiday, so we found ourselves in front of closed shops in Lindau. We would surely have found a lido on the lake as well, but we headed back to the campsite anyway.

The supermarket on site only took cash, whereas you could pay by card without any problems in the beer garden. The supermarket's opening hours were pleasingly flexible. The WIFI left something to be desired, unfortunately it was pretty poor. The overnight stay cost us CHF 54.04 per night.

And the most important thing at the end: next time, definitely pack clips for fastening tarps!
