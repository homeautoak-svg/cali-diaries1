From 27 to 30 August 2026 we stayed with our T7 California at Campingplatz Allensbach, right on the lake. The site is small to medium-sized, with pitches on grass or bare ground. Ours was under a big tree in the front row by the lake, a dream, we thought.

The lake itself is currently very flat and shallow, as the water level has dropped sharply due to the drought. Even so, windsurfers from the attached surf school were out on the water again and again. The beach consists of coarse gravel and stones, but it was comfortable enough to walk on in flip-flops.

Weather-wise it was a real rollercoaster: on Friday we had heavy rain and even a storm. Luckily we reacted quickly, retracted the awning and stowed all loose items in the Cali. That is exactly our lesson learnt: in a storm, act fast and get everything into the car in good time.

Saturday made up for it with perfect T-shirt weather, not too hot, ideal for a run into the beautiful hinterland via Kaltbrunn and the Mühlhaldenweiher pond. The storm had, however, blown dust and sand into every nook of the Cali and our gear, so most of Saturday morning went on cleaning.

Because public transport had largely been cancelled due to the storm, our trip to Konstanz was only possible at all thanks to the Cali. In the afternoon we headed to Allensbach, where we passed two large playgrounds that our daughter loved.

The campsite itself has a restaurant serving burgers and curries, a bakery, WIFI and a playground for the little guests. In the evening we sat with lots of other campers around a fire pit right on the beach, a lovely, sociable atmosphere with a mixed crowd.

All in all a great campsite that we are happy to recommend.
