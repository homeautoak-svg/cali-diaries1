From 7 to 9 August 2026 we made ourselves at home at the TCS campsite in Sempach. Our pitch was nice and roomy, so we could park our T7 California without any trouble and roll out the awning without stepping on the neighbours' toes. The sky was cloudless and it was a hot 32 degrees, perfect for sitting outside (in the shade).

There is a Coop just nearby that we could reach on foot. If you want to shop on a Saturday, though, be aware that it is only open until 17:00. On the campsite itself there was a lovely ice cream van with iced coffee, waffles and ice cream, which, to be honest, we visited more often than we would like to admit.

In the evening we went to the restaurant on the campsite. There was live music, which made for a great atmosphere. The food was hearty home cooking, but there was also a salad bar, so there was something for everyone. We spent around 55 francs on food and drinks together, and the overnight rate was 73.60 francs per night.

With the lake, a restaurant, WIFI and a supermarket nearby, the site really had everything you need for a relaxed weekend. We give it 4 out of 5 points.

There are a few things we will take with us for next time. At night it got quite chilly at around 10 degrees, so extra blankets are a must. An empty backpack for day trips and visits to the lido also turned out to be a good idea, and we want to plan for one from now on. And last but not least, the lamps must on no account stay in the cupboard at home on the next trip, because without light in the evening things quickly get uncomfortable.

All in all, Sempach was a thoroughly successful short trip with the Cali, and we will definitely be back.
