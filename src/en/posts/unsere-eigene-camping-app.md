Alongside this blog there is a second project running in the background, which actually got everything else started: our private camping logbook. An app we built for ourselves to record and plan our trips with the California.

The app accompanies us through the whole process around a trip. Before we set off, it helps with planning, with a meal plan, shopping list, packing list and an overview of open tasks, plus a weather forecast and the estimated driving distance from home. After the trip we write a trip report with a rating, costs, pitch size, photos and the lessons we took away.

Particularly useful is the collection of all lessons learnt across every trip. When the same question comes up again next time, such as which equipment has proved its worth or what we forgot when packing, we no longer have to rack our brains, we simply look it up.

On top of that there are a few practical tools: the mileage with an annual budget to match the lease, the service and repair history for the vehicle, a financial overview with fixed costs and automatically imported variable expenses, and a map showing all the pitches we have stayed at so far.

The app has become a permanent part of every trip for us, from the very first planning step to looking back afterwards.
