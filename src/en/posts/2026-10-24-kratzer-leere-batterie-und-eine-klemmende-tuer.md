We already hinted at it in our season round-up: on the way back from our last trip, we had to pull over for oncoming traffic on a narrow road and clipped the side barrier. The result was a nasty set of scratches running the entire length of the Cali's right-hand side. We reported the damage to the garage, which came to around CHF 4,000, luckily covered by our fully comprehensive insurance.

## A Sunday without power

Before we could even get the Cali to the garage, the next surprise came along. One Sunday, the 12-volt battery was completely flat, after just a week of standing still. We tried charging it for several hours, but eventually time ran out and we called the TCS.

The service technician managed to jump-start the Cali. To get to the 12-volt battery, though, we had to pull out all the carpets in the driver's area and remove several covers. He advised us to have the garage refit these parts properly.

On top of that, the rear right door wouldn't close all the way anymore. It would shut up to a certain point and then jam. For the drive to the garage, we fixed it in place with cable ties. On Monday morning, we called the garage and reported the new problems.

## A week later

On Friday afternoon, we got the call we'd been waiting for: the side door had been resprayed, the scratches were gone, the battery was charged, and the door closed again without jamming. Since picking the Cali up, it's been sitting in our garage fully functional once more.

## What we're taking away from this

- Slow right down on narrow roads. Better to crawl through safely than to end up getting the whole side resprayed afterwards.
- Move the Cali regularly. For us, just a week of standing still was enough to drain the 12-volt battery.
- Activate camper mode. We read in a forum that many systems and sensors keep drawing power from the 12-volt battery even after the vehicle is switched off.
- Keep a battery-powered jump starter at home. That would have saved us the Sunday charger marathon and the call to the TCS.
- Report every problem to the garage straight away, not just the original damage.

## Our plan for winter

Originally, we were just going to leave the Cali sitting in the garage over winter. We're changing that now: from now on, we'll use the Cali for shopping trips and the odd errand, ideally drives of about half an hour, to keep the battery charged. We'll see how well we manage that until things properly kick off again in spring.
