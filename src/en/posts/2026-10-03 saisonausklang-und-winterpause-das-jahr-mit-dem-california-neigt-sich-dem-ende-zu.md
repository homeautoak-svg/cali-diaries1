## A season in review

Our first full season with the California is drawing to a close. What began in August with a mix of anticipation and open questions now ends with a whole backpack full of experiences. Eight trips later we can say: the year was intense, varied and exactly what we had hoped for.

From the quiet nights at Lake Sempach and the heat and gravel at Gitzenweiler Hof to the storms in Allensbach, the Cali reliably took us everywhere. The golden September days stand out in particular: the cold mountain nights by the Vorderrhein in Disentis, the relaxed weekend in Kreuzlingen and finally the late summer calm on Lago Maggiore in Feriolo. Each of these stops showed us a different side of travelling as a family, from planning and improvising to simply being.

What made this season especially valuable for us was not just the travelling itself, but also slowing down. The Cali made us think differently: drive more slowly, fit less into less time, and actually use the time rather than just driving through it.

## The reality: a dent and a few scratches

On the way home from our last trip, however, reality caught up with us. On a narrow mountain road with oncoming traffic we were pushed into the crash barrier. The result was a few proper dents and scratches that have left their mark on the Cali. Frustrating on the one hand, but also a reminder that trips like these have their limits and risks. We will have the damage repaired and restore the Cali to its former glory.

## Winter break: planning for what comes next

We will use the coming months to prepare for next season. In concrete terms, that means researching good pitches and campsites and booking early. The high season does not wait, and anyone who does not plan early will be disappointed in summer.

We have also learnt a lot from this season. Which places did we like? Where would we like to stay longer? Which regions do we still want to explore? The winter break is the perfect time to answer these questions and make a better plan for 2027. Because if there is one thing we have understood, it is that the best moments often happen where you leave a little room for improvisation.

Until then the California will stay in the garage, repaired and serviced. Then we will be off again in spring.
